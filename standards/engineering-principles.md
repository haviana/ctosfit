# Engineering principles

1. **Correctness first** — satisfy documented behavior and acceptance criteria.
2. **Simplicity** — choose the least complex design that meets real requirements.
3. **Cohesion and boundaries** — keep responsibilities explicit and modules loosely coupled.
4. **Maintainability** — optimize for readable code and predictable change.
5. **Security and privacy by default** — minimize trust, data collection and permissions.
6. **Testability** — design seams for deterministic unit and integration tests.
7. **Observability** — expose useful metrics, logs and traces without leaking sensitive data.
8. **Resilience** — define timeouts, retries, idempotency and failure behavior intentionally.
9. **Compatibility** — consider API, data and deployment compatibility before changing contracts.
10. **Operational ownership** — document configuration, runbooks, recovery and support expectations.
11. **Evidence-based decisions** — record significant trade-offs and validate assumptions.
12. **Human accountability** — AI agents assist; maintainers own decisions and production outcomes.
