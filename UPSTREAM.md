# Engineering Umbrella Adoption

- Upstream: https://github.com/haviana/ai-development-umbrella
- Downstream: https://github.com/haviana/ctosfit
- Adopted by: `chore/adopt-ai-development-umbrella`
- Adoption model: curated snapshot, adapted to this product; not a live submodule or automatic sync.
- Human approval: required for consequential changes and merges.

## Update policy
1. Review upstream changes and their compatibility with Ctosfit.
2. Adapt rather than blindly copy; preserve product invariants in `AGENTS.md`.
3. Record the upstream commit or release reviewed and summarize material differences below.
4. Open a PR; do not overwrite local project-specific rules without review.

## Adoption notes
Initial adoption establishes local agent roles, skills, standards, governance, PR guidance and safe GitHub Actions workflows. The project-specific instructions in `AGENTS.md` take precedence for Ctosfit domain and architecture decisions.
