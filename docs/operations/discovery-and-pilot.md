# Discovery and Pilot Plan

## Discovery objectives

The [RegyBox 2.0 workflow review for WOD-9](regybox-workflow-review.md) is a
competitive input only; it does not replace coach/athlete interviews or pilot
evidence.

Validate how coaches plan/publish workouts, where programming and result tracking create friction, which tools are already used, whether historical programming influences decisions, and whether boxes will adopt/pay for a focused workout platform.

## Interview plan
Target 10 owners/coaches. Ask for concrete recent examples, not feature wish lists:
1. Walk me through planning and publishing last week's programming.
2. Which steps take the most time or cause mistakes?
3. How do athletes view workouts and submit results?
4. How do you track PRs, benchmarks and training history?
5. How do you review movement frequency or volume across a cycle?
6. Which tools must this product coexist with?
7. What would make you switch or add another tool?
8. What would make it not worth paying for?
9. Who decides and pays for software?
10. Would you test a prototype with a real class, and what must be true?

Record evidence, current workaround, frequency, severity and willingness to pilot. Separate observed pain from requested features.

## Design partners
Recruit 3–5 pilot boxes. Agree named owner/coach contacts, pilot duration, weekly feedback, real workflows to test, permitted data imports, support channel, export/exit expectations and what happens after any free pilot.

## Pilot scenarios
**Coach:** create/select exercises; build multi-block workout and variants; save draft, schedule and publish; reuse template; correct draft without changing historical publication.

**Athlete:** open workout on phone; select variant; log applicable score; review history and PR.

**Owner:** invite/remove members; assign roles; verify box-scoped access; export core data.

## Metrics
Measure time from draft to publication, scheduled-workout publication rate, athlete logging rate, median time-to-log, weekly active coaches/athletes, coach and box retention, support requests/workflow failures, and security/isolation defects.

## Pilot exit criteria
Continue if coaches repeatedly use programming, athletes log results with low friction, the product is reliable/secure and at least some boxes show credible willingness to pay. Positive comments alone do not constitute validation.
