# Approval Workflow

Agents may inspect, plan, implement on a branch and open a PR within the approved task scope.

Human approval is required for production changes, destructive operations, irreversible data migrations, new paid services or external integrations, credential/data-sharing changes, authentication/authorization/tenancy policy changes, material product or architecture changes, significant runtime/dependency upgrades, and merging or bypassing required checks.

The implementer cannot approve their own PR. Approval must come from an authorized human reviewer.
